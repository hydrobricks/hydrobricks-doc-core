Detailed Description

Seasonal degree-day refreezing of the snowpack liquid water (PREVAH).

As 'refreeze:degree_day', but with its own seasonal degree-day factor instead of reading it from a melt:degree_day sibling — PREVAH refreezes with the seasonal PDDI factor (CRMFMIN/CRMFMAX) on cold days even when the melt itself uses the Hock radiation equation (wstar = f_melting_eq(2, season_mf) * CRFR): RF = cfr x (mean + amp x sin(2 pi (doy - 80)/366)) x (T0 - T) for T < T0

The seasonal sine is identical to PREVAH's f_season_fact. Use it whenever the melt process is not a degree-day one (e.g. 'melt:temperature_index').
